How a project runs

To begin, send the brief by email, on Upwork or on Fiverr. I reply with an honest scope: what fits your budget and your date, and what does not.

Before any screen, the API contract is typed and agreed, so the web app, the mobile app and the backend build against one definition and cannot drift apart.

Every change is tested in CI before it ships. ETG carries 30,000+ tests; Sportefy’s Flutter app carries 823.

During your working day, I am online in your hours: Karachi is on UTC+5 and I overlap with US, EU and Australian teams, and progress reaches you in writing.

At the end, it is deployed on your domain or released to the App Store and the Play Store, with handover notes for whoever runs it next.
